Design a complete modern B2B marketplace web application called:

"Source. Build. Sell."

The platform is a B2B sourcing and manufacturing marketplace designed specifically for small and new D2C/online businesses.

CORE IDEA:
Small businesses often struggle to find reliable manufacturers, low-MOQ suppliers, product samples, customization, packaging, branding materials, and logistics.

Our platform connects:

BUYERS / D2C BUSINESS OWNERS
        ↕
VERIFIED MANUFACTURERS / SUPPLIERS

The long-term experience should be:

Product Idea
→ Find Manufacturer
→ Request Sample
→ Compare Quotes
→ Place Bulk Order
→ Production
→ Packaging
→ Shipping
→ Ready-to-Sell Product

MAIN TAGLINE:
"Source. Build. Sell."

IMPORTANT:
Design the product as a professional startup-grade B2B SaaS/marketplace platform.

Do NOT make it look like a basic e-commerce website.

It should feel trustworthy, modern, premium, clean, efficient and suitable for serious business users.

==================================================

==================================================

The final frontend will be implemented using:

- React

==================================================
UX PRINCIPLES
==================================================

The platform must reduce complexity for first-time entrepreneurs.

Use clear language.

Avoid technical B2B terminology where possible.

Instead of:

"Create RFQ"

Prefer:

"Post a Requirement"

Instead of:

"Supplier Discovery"

Prefer:

"Find Manufacturers"

Instead of:

"MOQ"

Display:

"Minimum Order"

and optionally show:

"MOQ: 100 pieces"

The user should always understand:

- What happens next?
- How much does it cost?
- What is the minimum quantity?
- Is the manufacturer verified?
- Can I get a sample?
- How long will production take?
